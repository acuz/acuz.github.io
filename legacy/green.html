Green Products Classification Tools

Companion resources for “Innovation, Green Products and Trade: Evidence from Multiproduct Firms” by Laura Bisio, Angelo Cuzzola, Marco Grazzi & Daniele Moschella
This classification was constructed manually by examining HS 2002 6-digit product descriptions and grouping green products with technologically similar conventional alternatives. The groupings involve subjective judgment about production processes, end-uses, and input requirements. Product descriptions are the official HS 2002 6-digit texts. The environmental category of each group is the one under which the source lists (OECD, OECD2, APEC, WTO) include its green codes; the HS nomenclature does not distinguish, for instance, bio-based from conventional plastics. This tool is provided for transparency and to facilitate community feedback.

About these classifications. A product is flagged as green if its HS6 code appears on at least one of four international policy lists, as consolidated by Mealy & Teytelboym (2022, Research Policy): the WTO Doha Core List (2001), the OECD Combined List (1999), an extended OECD list (OECD2), and the APEC List of Environmental Goods (2012). The dataset aggregates 525 HS6 candidates evaluated under at least one of these processes (drawn from the broader WTO Reference Universe considered during the Doha negotiations and from products debated by the OECD and APEC working groups). Of these candidates, 301 appear on at least one final list and constitute the green-product classification used in our paper; the remaining 224 were considered but excluded. All codes and descriptions use the HS 2002 6-digit nomenclature.
